## 🎯 Product Page Optimization

### Add These Sections to Each Product
1. **Detailed Specifications Table**
2. **"What's in the Box"** section
3. **FAQs** specific to that product
4. **Use Cases/Applications**
5. **Customer Reviews** (with schema markup)
6. **Related Products**
7. **Video Demonstrations**

### Example FAQ Schema
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [{
    "@type": "Question",
    "name": "What materials can this printer use?",
    "acceptedAnswer": {
      "@type": "Answer",
      "text": "This printer supports PLA, ABS, PETG, and TPU filaments..."
    }
  }]
}
```

## 📍 Local SEO Strategies

### Google Business Profile
1. Create/claim your Google Business Profile
2. Add accurate business information
3. Upload photos of products and workspace
4. Encourage customer reviews
5. Post regular updates

### Local Citations
List your business on:
- JustDial
- IndiaMART
- TradeIndia
- Sulekha
- Yellow Pages India

### Location Pages (if applicable)
Create pages for:
- "3D Printing Services in [City]"
- "Buy 3D Printers in [City]"
- Add local landmarks, directions
- Include local phone numbers
